User Acknowledgement prior to use of CloudGavel

  1. By using CloudGavel, you acknowledge that you are accessing Criminal Justice Information and agree to abide by all of the local, state and federal requirements regarding the handling of Criminal Justice Information.
  2. By using CloudGavel, you acknowledge that you understand the system is being monitored, all activity is being recorded (logged) and is subject to periodic audits.
  3. By using CloudGavel, you acknowledge that unauthorized use is prohibited and may be subject to criminal and/or civil penalties.
  4. Your use of CloudGavel indicates your consent to monitoring and recording.

User Agreement

  1. It is the responsibility of the Client Agency to insure access to CloudGavel is for authorized purposes only, and to regulate the proper use of the network and information at all times. The Client must establish appropriate written standards, which may be incorporated with existing codes of conduct, for disciplining violators of this and any incorporated policy.
  2. If the User provides an interface between CloudGavel and other criminal justice agencies it must abide by all of the provisions of this agreement. Agencies that access CloudGavel systems by interfacing through other agencies must, likewise, abide by all provisions of this agreement. An inter-agency User Agreement is required when access to CloudGavel is provided by the User to another agency.
    1. MESSAGES: Only law enforcement and other criminal justice messages shall be sent over and through the CloudGavel. All messages will be treated as privileged unless otherwise indicated. User should make prudent use of regional and statewide broadcast message requests. All messages must use plain English text in the message.
    2. COMPLIANCE: Access to the CloudGavel application in strict compliance with applicable CJIS policies including, but not limited to, policies, practices and procedures relating to:
    3. TIMELINESS: CloudGavel records must be entered, modified, located, cleared; and canceled promptly in order to ensure system accuracy and effectiveness. Users that perform CloudGavel updates for other agencies must comply with timeliness requirements for the records entered for the serviced agencies as well.
    4. QUALITY ASSURANCE: Appropriate and reasonable quality assurance procedures must be in place, including second party verification during entry, to ensure all entries in CloudGavel are complete, accurate, and valid.
    5. DISSEMINATION: Information obtained from the CloudGavel or computer interfaces to any state or federal systems can only be used for official criminal justice purposes.
      The user will disseminate CJI derived from or generated by the use of CloudGavel only to criminal justice agencies or appropriate judicial agencies and only for criminal justice purposes.
    6. RETENTION: CJI, regardless of how the CJI is obtained or generated, must be kept in a secure records environment to prevent unauthorized access.
    7. CJI TRANSMISSION: A facsimile machine or other electronic means (i.e. - email) may be used to transmit criminal history information between criminal justice agencies, provided both agencies are authorized to receive criminal justice information. Appropriate measures must be taken to prevent unauthorized viewing or receipt by unauthorized persons.
    8. LOGGING: Each authorized user of the CloudGavel system will have an automated log kept by CloudGavel for an indefinite period of time.
      1. An automated logging feature is included in the application software provided by CloudGavel and CloudGavel will retain these logs for future reference by the client agencies or for CJIS compliance audits.
      2. The automated transaction log shall identify: the operator on all transactions, the agency authorizing all transactions, any secondary recipient for all CJI transactions. This information shall be captured at log-on and shall be attached to all actions taken within the CloudGavel system.
      3. The User may only disseminate information to another authorized recipient and a record shall be maintained of any dissemination of CJI. This record will reflect at a minimum: (1) date of release; (2) to whom the information relates; (3) to whom the information was released.
    9. INFORMATION ACCESS: The User will allow only properly screened, authorized personnel performing a criminal justice function who have received proper security training to have access to information contained within the CloudGavel system.
      1. The User will ensure that all personnel who initiate a transaction are current in CJIS certification. The User will ensure that persons allowed to complete CJIS certification are at least 18 years of age and are U.S. citizens or have a valid immigration status/visa.
      2. CloudGavel reserves the right to deny CloudGavel systems access to any individual based on valid, articulable concerns for the security and integrity of CloudGavel systems.
    10. WORKSTATION: CloudGavel is not responsible for the workstation acquisition, maintenance, operation, repair, supplies or workstation operation personnel costs. CloudGavel, at its own discretion, may assist with executing trouble-shooting procedures to ensure proper operation of the CloudGavel system.
    11. AUDITS: All agencies having access to the CloudGavel system shall permit an inspection team to conduct inquiries with regard to any allegations or potential security violations, as well as for routine audits.
      1. CloudGavel conducts regularly scheduled compliance and technical security audits of every agency accessing the system to ensure network security, conformity with state law, and compliance with all applicable CJIS rules, regulations and operating procedures.
      2. Compliance and technical security audits may be conducted at other than regularly scheduled times.
      3. Security audits of user data and user logs may be conducted at other than regularly scheduled times.
    12. TRAINING: The User is responsible for complying with training requirements established in CJIS Security Policy and the rules, regulations, and policies. The User is responsible for remaining current in the applications, procedures, and policies and ensuring personnel attend these training sessions.
      1. CloudGavel operators who are in their initial six months of assignment may be permitted supervised access to CloudGavel. Operators must successfully complete CJIS certification within six months of appointment or assignment to duties requiring direct access to CloudGavel.
      2. The User will require all personnel who initiate a transaction in the CloudGavel system to successfully complete CJIS Certification. The User agrees to remove from CloudGavel access any employee who fails to achieve required certification standards, whose certification has expired, whose certificate is otherwise rescinded or as directed by in accordance with CJIS policy.
      3. The User will maintain certification records of all CJIS certified personnel in a current status.

  3. Security Requirements
    1. PERSONNEL BACKGROUND SCREENING and POLICY FOR DISCIPLINE: The User is required to conduct a background investigation on all personnel who are authorized to access CloudGavel and CJI data or systems, IT personnel who maintain/support information technology components used to process, and other personnel accessing workstation areas that are unescorted by authorized personnel.
      Good management practices dictate the investigation should be completed prior to employment, but it must, at a minimum, be conducted within the first thirty (30) days of employment or assignment. The User may conduct a preliminary on-line criminal justice employment check.
    2. The User must submit applicant fingerprints for positive comparison against the state and national criminal history. If a record of any kind is found, the User will not permit the operator to have access to the CloudGavel system. The User will formally notify the CJIS Systems Officer (CSO) indicating access will be delayed pending review of the criminal history.
      1. When identification of the applicant has been established by fingerprint comparison and the applicant appears to be a fugitive, have pending criminal charges; have an arrest history for a felony or serious misdemeanor; have been found guilty of, regardless of adjudication, or entered a plea of nolo contendere or guilty to any felony or serious misdemeanor; or to tie under the supervision of the court, the User will refer the matter to the CSO for review.
      2. Applicants who have been found guilty of, regardless of adjudication, or entered a plea of nolo contendere or guilty to a felony, will generally be denied access to CloudGavel. For applicants who have been adjudicated guilty of or have had adjudication withheld on a misdemeanor, the User will notify the CSO of the misdemeanor(s) and acknowledge an intent to permit CloudGavel access. Access will also generally be denied to any person under court supervision for a criminal offense or against whom criminal charges are pending. If a determination is made by the CSO that CloudGavel access by the applicant would not be in the public interest, such access will be denied and the User will be notified in writing of the access denial.
      3. Once the original background screening has been completed, if the User learns that a CJIS certified employee has a criminal history or pending charge(s), the User will first determine if the pending charge(s) or record(s) adversely affect(s) the employee's continued employment/access/status with the User. If the employee is placed on leave, terminated or denied access to CloudGavel by the User, as a result of any pending or unresolved charge(s), the User will notify the CSO of the action taken. Generally, denial of access, pending satisfactory resolution of any such charge(s), and notification to the CSO of that action, will be deemed sufficient corrective action by the User.
      4. If the User determines that no action is required while the charge(s) are pending or unresolved; i.e., that the employee will continue to be allowed access to CloudGavel, the User will notify the CSO and explain its rationale for continued access. CloudGavel reserves the right, as CSA, to deny access to CloudGavel and associated databases until any such charge(s) are resolved or the situation is clarified.
      5. The User will have a written policy for discipline of personnel who access CloudGavel for purposes that are not authorized, disclose information to unauthorized individuals, or violate CJIS rules, regulations or operating procedures
    3. PHYSICAL SECURITY: The User will determine the perimeter for the physical security of devices that access or provide access to CloudGavel.
      1. Access shall be limited as to allow completion of required duties.
      2. The User must have a written policy that ensures and implements security measures, secures devices that access CloudGavel and prevents unauthorized use or viewing of information on these devices.
      3. The use of screen blanking-software with password protection is recommended for devices that access CloudGavel when the operator may leave the computer unsupervised. CloudGavel reserves the right to object to equipment location, security measures and qualifications of personnel who will be accessing CloudGavel and to suspend or withhold service until such matters are corrected to its reasonable satisfaction.
    4. ADMINISTRATIVE SECURITY: The User must designate at least one individual agency contact to assist the User and CloudGavel with the information services covered by this agreement. Training for these positions is provided by CloudGavel, and the User must ensure that its designee is keenly aware of the duties and responsibilities of each respective position. The User is required to provide CloudGavel with up-to-date contact information for the System Administrator.
    5. TECHNICAL SECURITY:
      1. Dial-up services to CloudGavel will be permitted provided the User establishes appropriate security measures to ensure compliance with all rules, regulations, procedures, and the FBI CJIS Security Policy.
      2. All CloudGavel data transmitted over any public network segment must be encrypted as required by tire FBI CJIS Security Policy. This requirement also applies to any private data circuit that is shared with non-criminal justice users and/or is not under the direct security control of a criminal justice agency.
      3. The User must ensure all devices with connectivity to CloudGavel employ virus protection software and such software shall be maintained in accordance with the software vendor's published updates.
      4. To ensure appropriate security precautions are in place, and upon approval from CloudGavel, the User may employ wireless network connectivity (for example the 802.11 wireless networking protocol).
    6. COMPUTER SECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE CAPABILITY: The User must have a written policy documenting the actions to be taken in response to a possible computer security incident. The policy should include identifying, reporting, investigating and recovery from computer security incidents. The User will immediately notify CloudGavel of any suspected compromise of the CloudGavel system.
      1. SECURITY AUTHORITY: All policies, procedures and operating instructions. contained in the FBI CJIS' Security Policy and FCIC/NCIC, Ill and NLETS - documents, operating manuals and technical memoranda, are hereby incorporated into and made a part of this agreement, except to the extent that they are inconsistent herewith or legally superseded by higher authority.

The Standard Service Agreement contains all of the limitations, liabilities and responsibilities of both parties (the Client Agency and CloudGavel) regarding the use of the CloudGavel software.

      4. INDIVIDUAL USER ACCESS: The User will deactivate individual user access to CloudGavel upon separation, reassignment or termination of duties, provided individual user access is no longer required for the administration of criminal justice.
